1. **`agents/codex-reviewer.md`'s spec-review-mode "Route-back" paragraph resolves the answering author from a condition source this task's own surrounding text already knows is unreliable at this exact seam, and never cross-checks it.** The paragraph reads: "route the findings back to the spec's own author, resolved through the `specify` decision **recorded on the task's board entry**." Direct empirical confirmation: every board entry with dispatch records sampled in this checkout (T-1092's own entry, T-1089, T-1087, T-1091, and others) transcribes its `- dispatch:` sub-bullets **strictly after** its `- intent-hash (vN)` line — never before. The Specify-seam spec-review gate fires only **before** the intent-hash is appended (both this task's own Goal and `skills/run/SKILL.md`'s placement require this). So at the one point this codex-reviewer mode ever runs, the board's `- dispatch: specify —` sub-bullet its own Route-back paragraph names as the resolution source is, by this repository's own demonstrated and universal sequence, guaranteed not to exist yet. Separately, even where a stale or mistyped `specify` sub-bullet from a *previous* task's entry template happened to be present, the paragraph never cross-checks it against `pm-spec`'s hand-off or the Routing Map's printed decision the way the Specify-seam gate itself is required to (`agents/pm-spec.md`'s own Route-back cross-check discipline: "in either direction... a mismatch... refuses"), so a wrong board value would silently misroute a `REQUEST_CHANGES` to the wrong party rather than refuse.
