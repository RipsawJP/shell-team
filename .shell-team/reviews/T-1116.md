**Finding 3 (calibration-to-plugin cross-role confound) — upheld as Major, bounded to the conservative failure direction.** This confound is real and not disclosed anywhere in the frozen text: Input-space item 4 only discloses a *non-conformance* risk (a garbled reply voiding a rep, pushing toward `undetermined`), not a *conforming-but-systematically-shifted-difficulty* risk under `tech-lead`'s own system prompt (both tiers pushed to a common floor or ceiling for reasons unrelated to which model ran). Worked through the failure direction by hand: because a `pin-governs` scenario has all three plugin arms literally running the identical model+role+prompt, any divergence between `plug-haiku` and `plug-opus` under that hypothesis is pure sampling noise already priced by DP-3 — so this confound cannot manufacture a false `routed-on-plugin-role` (Branch A, the consequential branch). Genuinely undisclosed and worth fixing, but its practical blast radius is bounded to under-detecting rather than over-claiming routing.

**Finding 4 (threshold-arithmetic overclaim) — upheld as Major.** Independently re-derived: the "joint 1/4096" figure answers "what is the chance the *entire* protocol (calibration admission AND plugin differential) separates by pure chance if literally nothing discriminates anything," a valid worst-case sanity bound. Prior-review-row-2's own disposition text states "closed by design... 1/4096 ≈ 0.024%" without this distinction, which overstates the operative protection by roughly two orders of magnitude for the reader deciding how much to trust a positive result. The underlying threshold *choice* (N=3, unanimity) remains the best achievable at this sample size and is not itself defective — the fix is a clarification of which figure answers which question, not a redesign.
