#### Minor / nits
- minor — `agents/code-reviewer.md:56` (and the same wording at the evidence, spec-review and drift-evaluator await paragraphs) — `turn.completed` releases `publish` without confirming the launched task exited or that `-o` was flushed. Measured here: with a background launch, the `-o` capture was absent at the first poll that saw `turn.completed` and present about a second later, so `-o` is written after the terminal event. The harness's own `[exited with code N]` line follows the stream. — suggested fix: after `turn.completed`, have the role wait for that exit line (or retry `publish` once after a short pause on exit 3/4) before treating a publish refusal as a failed pass.
- minor — `bin/codex-await.sh:128`, `:145`, `:153` — each poll collects every grep-matched candidate line into one variable and walks it in bash before the deadline check, so the T-row's "no later than five seconds after the timeout" bound degrades with candidate count. Measured here: 800 candidate lines in a 12 MB stream with a 3 s timeout exited after 4 s (inside the bound); 20 000 candidate lines (1.4 MB) exited after 13 s. Only a stream with thousands of event-name mentions reaches it. — suggested fix: check the deadline inside the candidate loop, or cap the candidates read per poll.
- minor — `agents/code-reviewer.md` Finding-evaluation mode paragraph (about line 174) — says to collect exactly as the primary pass's `codex` block rule prescribes, whose stop attempt identifies the process by `<RAW_OUT>`, but this mode has no `alloc`/`-o` and so no `<RAW_OUT>`. The role is left to improvise how to identify the process (the risk being a name-based match on `codex`). — suggested fix: say that in this mode the stop attempt matches a path-free token unique to the invocation, or is skipped and reported as not attempted.
- nit — `bin/codex-await.sh:116`, `:118` — the matcher is a whole-line, key-position pattern, not JSON validation: a well-keyed line with a malformed tail inside the trailing `[^{}]*` / `.*` still counts, and an end event is recognised only with `type` first or last. Real Codex output is compact with `type` first, and measured quoted, embedded and trailing-text cases are all rejected.
- nit — `bin/codex-await.sh:166` — the timeout tail is printed unredacted; it is the same bytes the role's own tool result carries, so it adds no disclosure channel.
- nit — `bin/codex-await.sh:114`, `:115` — classes inherit the caller's locale; measured here under the C locale and two Unicode locales (English and Japanese), a non-ASCII id is rejected in all three.
- nit — the stop instruction (`agents/code-reviewer.md:46`) says never end the turn while a pass is unresolved and also forbids retrying a refused `pkill`; read together they mean a refused stop is reported and the pass is then resolved as `BLOCKED` with a possibly still-running orphan. That is honest and bounded, but the sentence does not say so.
