#### Blockers

- **`bin/check-review-input.sh:365-390` — the `self-detected-host` value's own leading whitespace is never normalized before the `claude-code`/`codex-cli` vocabulary comparison, so a syntactically legitimate record is falsely refused.** `FIELD_RE` (`:262`) consumes exactly one optional space after the field's colon (`:[[:space:]]?`); any additional whitespace the author typed survives into the captured value untouched — the identical shape `.shell-team/specs/T-1138-reviewer-host-self-classification.md:245` (`## Notes for engineer`, Gotcha 1) names explicitly: "Normalize it before taking the two tokens (D4, AC3's second arm), **and normalize it before testing the host token too**." The `executor-invocation` side of the contradiction check does this (the `ei_norm` strip loop at `:399-405`); the `self-detected-host` side does not — `sdh_val` is split on ` — ` and compared to the closed pair with no equivalent strip. Live-reproduced: a record with `- self-detected-host (primary):  claude-code — CODEX_THREAD_ID was not set` (two spaces after the colon, one consumed by `FIELD_RE`, one surviving) exits 1 with `self-detected-host-vocabulary`, even though the record is exactly as well-formed as the accepted single-space form. This is squarely the false-refusal direction the spec's own Pre-commitment prices as worse than silence ("a refusal that fires on a legitimate record ... is a worse outcome than no refusal at all" — never-dropped alongside AC2-AC6), and it is an explicit, named engineering directive for this exact field that was not carried out, not merely an inherited quirk shared incidentally with `pass-role` (confirmed live against the base blob: `pass-role` has the identical latent behaviour today, but that field is untouched by this diff and out of this task's scope per `## Non-goals`; `self-detected-host` is this diff's own deliverable, and its own spec text calls for the fix by name).
  - Suggested fix: strip leading whitespace/tabs from `sdh_val` (mirroring the `ei_norm` loop, or a simple `sdh_val="${sdh_val#"${sdh_val%%[![:space:]]*}"}"`-style trim) before the ` — ` split, and add a regression fixture with 2+ spaces after the colon to `tests/check-review-input/run.sh` (currently untested by any of the 11 new arms).
