   literally (mktemp is present; nothing is composed). The alternative (inventing a fifth,
   off-ladder "not applicable" category) is explicitly rejected in the provenance reasoning, and
   correctly so — DP2 defines exactly four classes. **Sound.**
3. **Bare `"$d"` reference ≠ composed path.** DP2 rung (2) uses the verb "composed... into... a

   example (`"$d/raw.txt"` expanding to `/raw.txt`) is a segment-appended-to-empty-string hazard,
   materially different from a bare `cd ""` (which fails outright rather than silently
   root-anchoring). **Sound**, and consistent with the audit document's own "composed path" Terms
   definition.
