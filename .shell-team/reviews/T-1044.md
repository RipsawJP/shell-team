  two-branch idiom.

  The suite's relative-symlink launch-path case hardcodes a 4-hop
  `../../../../` relative path from `linkdir` to `REPO_ROOT/bin`. Reproduced
  directly: creating the identical symlink shape under `$TMPDIR` (outside the
  repo tree) and resolving it fails with "no such file or directory" — the
  exact failure the provenance record describes, matching the depth math
  (a `$TMPDIR`-rooted directory is not 4 hops from `REPO_ROOT`).
