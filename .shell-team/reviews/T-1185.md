Probe: with `.shell-team/todo.md` committed and `/.shell-team/` in `info/exclude`, an edit to todo.md shows as modified but a new `.shell-team/new.md` is hidden. Ignore rules do not apply to files git already tracks, and still hide new untracked ones; line 83 (EN and JA) says only the modified half. Fix: state both halves in both languages — tracked records stay visible and editable-as-modified, new untracked records are hidden — and say who therefore must decide what to do with the tracked ones. Found by the primary pass (P2) and confirmed by the reviewer's own probe.

Line 56 is correctly conditioned ("as long as nobody commits the base dir"); line 40 is not. Fix: condition step 5 on a base dir that is not in HEAD, and point at the next section for the committed case. Found by the adversarial pass; confirmed by the reviewer against bin/team-setup.sh:797 and the section's own line 50.
