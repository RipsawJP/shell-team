The corpus-reading criteria (which *do* correctly re-resolve through the resolver at run time) would then look in the resolver's real answer, find nothing staged there, and reproduce the exact base-arm false-FAIL bug this whole task exists to eliminate — for exactly the adopter layouts the spec's own Input space bullet 5 declares in scope ("An adopter repository on the default layout, on the legacy layout, or on a custom `TEAM_RUN_BASE` — which is why every criterion and the norm text resolve the corpus through `bin/team-paths.sh --get runs` rather than naming `.shell-team/runs` literally").
