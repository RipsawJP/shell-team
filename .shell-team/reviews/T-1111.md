### My own synthesis of Codex's findings

**Finding 1 (discriminator evidential validity) — upheld as Blocker, and this is the load-bearing finding of this round.** This directly matches my own top-priority attack instruction. The spec's text (`## Goal`, "The discriminator") asserts a uniform `absent`/`refused` result is "**harness-originated** evidence" — but the report is mediated entirely through the probed instance's own text output, which is exactly the self-attestation boundary `agent-launch-fanout.md:247`'s own counterfactual already disclosed for `subagent_type` itself — the spec does not disclose that its own "decisive" discriminator sits on the identical unverifiable footing as the very gap it exists to close. **A-7** discloses *one* narrow slice of this ("whether a plugin-role instance reports a missing Write tool as `absent` rather than failing some third way is unknown") but not the deeper confound: a sandbox-level write denial that applies uniformly regardless of `agents/*.md` role would produce the identical `refused`/`absent` signature as a genuine role-scoped tool restriction, and nothing in the frozen protocol distinguishes the two causes. This compounds with finding 5 below: if the clone venue genuinely enforces "read-only for every probe agent" at the filesystem level (as literally written), *every* instance — `qa-verifier` or `general-purpose` — would report `refused`/`absent` for reasons having nothing to do with role resolution, and the discriminator's decisive case (`plugin-role-confirmed`) becomes trivially, vacuously satisfiable. The droppable-first `mixed-type-n3` arm is the only design element that would let this be caught, and it is explicitly not required to run.

**Finding 2 (rep2 evidence gap) — upheld as Major.** Verified directly against **AC6**'s and **AC7**'s own `- check:` lines: both hard-code `resolved-type-n3-rep1` in their `grep` patterns and never require an equivalent count for `resolved-type-n3-rep2`. **AC5** only confirms both arms are logged `executed`, not that either carries real per-instance evidence. Since `repetition-variance` is one of the seven licence conditions **AC9** requires `met` for a positive verdict, and nothing mechanically forces rep2's own evidence into existence, a `repetition-variance — met` claim is unfalsifiable by these criteria alone.

**Finding 3 (unfrozen timing fields) — upheld as Major.** Confirmed: **AC8**'s check parses `unit-duration`/`launch-latency`/`margin-factor` as bare digits and checks only an inequality; unlike **AC6**'s overlap-margin re-derivation (which recomputes `margin_ns` from the raw `- agent-timestamp:` lines and rejects a mismatch), no criterion re-derives `unit-duration` or `launch-latency` from any raw, frozen capture. **Finding 4 (clock-source-monotonic mislabeling) — upheld as Major, but disposed as fast-follow against a repo-wide convention rather than blocking T-1111 specifically.** Verified: `time.time_ns()` reads the adjustable wall clock, not a monotonic source; Python's own `time.monotonic_ns()` is the correct primitive for a condition genuinely named "monotonic."
