The closed `unit-and-static` / `real-environment` pair is spec-level, but a spec's criteria can span more than one real-environment capability class at different cost (e.g. one criterion needs only a staging storage→queue→worker path, actually exercised; another needs a production deploy or credentialed work, not exercised).
