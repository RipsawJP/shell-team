## Problem

Three shipped documents each say something that is not true of the thing they describe, and all three were measured and recorded by a gate that then declined to fix them in place. `skills/run/SKILL.md`'s two durability-barrier bullets give an unconditional instruction while the neighbouring interventions gate three lines below explicitly carves out the no-task case — so an orchestrator in a no-task flow reads two adjacent instructions that disagree about whether their step applies (#198 item 1). `.shell-team/test-recipe.md` records the scratch-install shape for one checker whose registry resolves from its own script directory but not for its sibling, which resolves the same way and will fail the same way (#198 item 2). `docs/tuning-oversight.md` and its Japanese twin state a consequence-based criterion — does merging this change what an agent subsequently does — and then instantiate it with a path list that silently omits an advisory instruction source the criterion plainly reaches, leaving the boundary undecided rather than decided (#54). And the `K2` grep-scope convention, shipped in `agents/pm-spec.md` and frozen byte-identically inside two merged intent blocks, justifies `git grep` with a mechanism claim that is factually wrong: `git grep` **skips** a tracked symlink entry entirely, it does not read the link's own blob (#206).

The three share a shape and it is not the shape of a behaviour bug: each is a sentence whose truth nobody re-measures, sitting beside a mechanism that has not changed. Two of them — the K2 sites — sit inside merged frozen intent and can only move through a ratified class-B re-freeze; the rest are ordinary edits to mutable prose and to shipped skill text no marker region owns.
