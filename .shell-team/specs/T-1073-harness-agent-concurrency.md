**Unit.** The production unit `bash bin/check-acs.sh <spec>` over real committed specs in the pinned clone — the same unit the sibling width-axis pilot used, deliberately, so that agent-launch overhead becomes separable against a known shell-process ladder rather than confounded with it. The unit is never a synthetic sleep and never a toy command. **Frozen condition:** the unit's own duration must exceed the measured agent-launch latency by a declared margin factor of **at least 3**, and where it does not, the verdict is `undetermined` or `launch-refused` and no other verdict may be recorded. The factor is a stated choice rather than a measurement — a floor chosen so that a marginal ratio cannot license a positive verdict — and the note states it as such. The unit's duration must also sit **below** the watchdog's stall threshold, so that a healthy arm does not alert and the negative control's alert means something; where the threshold is fixed by the watchdog rather than tunable by the probe, the **population** is what moves — specs are chosen whose run cost falls in that band, before any arm runs. If no population on the probe host satisfies both bounds at once, that finding is itself recorded and the verdict is `undetermined`.

**Clock source.** The probe records which clock it read, and verifies it before relying on it: a command whose nanosecond field does not expand (BSD `date` does not implement `%N`, and emits a literal `N`) is **unusable**, and the probe substitutes a source that does expand and records which. **Evidence, three channels.** ① `agent-self-timestamps` — each agent reports its own first and last monotonic timestamps; this is the **primary** overlap evidence, because it is the only channel measured from inside the agents themselves. ② `batch-vs-sum` — the orchestrator's batch wall-clock against the sum of per-agent durations; a batch shorter than the sum by more than tolerance is a **necessary condition** for overlap, never sufficient on its own. ③ `orchestrator-span-rows` — span rows carrying `--instance probe-a|probe-b|probe-c|probe-d`, emitted **sequentially by the orchestrator after the completion notifications**, never by the probe agents themselves; this is **secondary, attribution-only** evidence, and the note records the observation that agent-level fan-out cannot self-emit its own spans until the append-locking car (#285) lands.

**Watchdog liveness evidence, never dropped.** The specific defect that defeated the sibling task's probe was a watchdog the parent never observed.
