## Problem

T-1089 stopped `BLOCKED` under operator route (c) after three consecutive cross-provider rounds landed the same `guard-weaker-than-prose` class on its own inline `- check:` lines (Majors 2 → 4 → 6) while QA passed every round. Five of round 3's six Majors are check-line-robustness findings that travel to issues #307 and #336; the sixth is different in kind and is the one thing on that list that is a defect in **shipped contract text** rather than in a guard: the subject brief's `- subject-ac: 8` obligation tells a candidate to read a pattern-matching field as a `10#`-normalized decimal integer, and that mechanism hint is not safe for the negative zero-padded spellings the frozen pattern `^-?[0-9]+$` admits. The stated outcome is right and the stated mechanism is the trap, so a candidate that follows the brief literally ships the exact class the closure exists to close — silently for one half of it and with a hard error for the other.

T-1090 is the approved minimal successor: fix that one real instrument defect in the two non-frozen places it lands, and take the preserved harness work through both gates under the operator's re-priced review scope for development-time scaffold specs.
