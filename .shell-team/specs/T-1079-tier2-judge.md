The two arms exist because the correct ref has two eras, both measured rather than assumed — the same reasoning shape `.shell-team/specs/T-1078-tier3-pilot.md` (lines 7–11) states for its own stack, re-anchored one car forward. **Today**, this task's branch is stacked on PR #305 (`feature/1078-tier3-pilot`), which is **open and unmerged**, and PRs #303 and #296 are open below it, so `develop`'s tip contains none of them: a bare `develop` spelling would put three open cars' files permanently inside every `<base>...HEAD` range, making this spec's byte-identity and scope-lock criteria structurally unsatisfiable rather than merely red. **After the batch GO**, the cars merge and their branches are deleted; `git merge-base develop HEAD` then resolves the same commit the first arm resolves today, because `develop` has come to contain it. The two-arm form is therefore not a hedge between two candidate refs — **both arms resolve the same commit, each in the era where it is the one that can**. The first arm is written as `git merge-base "$BR" HEAD` rather than as a `rev-parse` of the branch tip on purpose: a rework round that advances `feature/1078-tier3-pilot` after this branch was cut moves that branch's tip but not the common ancestor, and the common ancestor is what "branch point" means here.

The fallback is written as an explicit branch-existence test rather than as a `2>/dev/null ||` chain on purpose: a `||` chain cannot distinguish "the predecessor branch is gone" (the expected era change, which must fall back) from "`git merge-base` failed for some other reason" (which must fail closed), and collapsing the two would let a broken read silently resolve a different, earlier commit. Each arm's own failure still exits non-zero. One residual case is disclosed rather than engineered away: if the predecessor branch were deleted **without** merging — an abandoned car — the second arm would resolve `develop`'s tip, which is not this branch's point; that scenario invalidates the whole stack this task sits on, and is a route-back rather than something a criterion should paper over. No 40-hex literal is written into any criterion: every criterion resolves the ref itself and fails closed when it does not resolve.

### Verdict scope (operator-ratified)

**Authorship: operator-ratified.** The primary record is the `- intervention: human-correction` entry dated 2026-08-14 in `.shell-team/interventions/T-1066.md`. This section restates that ruling for the gates that read this spec; it is not this spec's self-discipline and it is not re-litigated here.

For this deliverable — a quantitative research note whose purpose is to inform a decision about restructuring how the loop spends machine time — **deliverable correctness means two things**, and a finding against either is verdict-gating: (1) the **computed values** are correct, meaning each figure names the source it was read from and reproduces when the command quoted beside it is re-run; and (2) the **conclusions' direction** is right, meaning what the note says its numbers support is what they support — the cost model's inequality, the saving's confidence word, the tier's recommendation and the comparison verdict.

**Connective prose is not verdict-gating.** A finding about a sentence that joins, summarises or characterises values that are themselves correct — a totality word whose domain is wider than its command's, a label mismatch, a stale count in a narrative aside, an imprecise summary — is recorded as a **note** in the review record and does not on its own produce `REQUEST_CHANGES` or `FAIL`, provided every computed value it touches is correct and no conclusion's direction moves. **No escalation ratchet is created over prose classes**: repeated findings of a connective-prose class do not raise a gate's severity, do not create a halt condition, and do not on their own trigger a rework round; they accumulate as notes and are dispositioned at close-out.

**D1 — no Tier-2 pilot arm runs, and the three measured grounds for that are stated rather than assumed.** This task designs on paper and models from recorded corpora; it launches no competing engineer instances. **(a)** The premise that an arm would be cheap because T-1078's subject task is reusable was measured **false**: that subject was never committed (T-1078's own Non-goals kept every pilot artefact under `$TMPDIR`), so no committed subject, baseline or harness exists and an arm would have to re-author and re-run a serial baseline first. **(b)** A Tier-2 wall-clock arm is structurally near-tautological: the parallel arm's wall clock is the maximum of N draws from the same distribution the serial arm draws once from, so it is `≥` the serial mean by construction, and measuring it would answer a question nobody asked. **(c)** The tier's actual bet is **rework-round reduction**, which is unmeasurable without real QA and review gates — the venue T-1078's D1(a) excluded on the ground that a live task's deliverable must not sit inside a measurement's blast radius. The route is recorded in `.shell-team/interventions/T-1079.md`. What (b) and (c) cost is stated rather than hidden: no figure in this task's cost model is observed live, which is exactly why no line it writes may read `measured`.

**D2 — the judge's own contract is designed as five closed vocabularies, not as prose.** Prose describing a role cannot be implemented without re-deciding it, and a later implementing task must not have to re-derive what this one settled. The design is therefore carried on five new labelled families, each closed in the note's own Terms section: `- judge-input:` (what the judge reads), `- judge-criterion:` (what it scores, with a total order), `- judge-output:` (the fields of the machine-parseable verdict line, each `required`, `optional` or `dropped`), `- judge-forbidden:` (what it may never do), and `- judge-bound:` (what bounds the merge half and the phase position). The verdict line's own grammar is quoted in Terms and **never instantiated**: no `- judge-pick: ` line exists anywhere in the note, because no judge has run and a specimen verdict would be indistinguishable from a fabricated one.

**D3 — what the judge reads, scores and emits.** It reads N candidate hand-offs, each candidate's diff against the one shared base every candidate started from, the frozen spec's own acceptance criteria, and the per-candidate `- check:` results — the last three `required`, the hand-off narrative `optional`, because a hand-off is a claim and the diff is the artefact. It scores over exactly four criteria, ranked in a total order so two candidates cannot tie on precedence: `ac-pass-completeness`, `test-depth`, `scope-discipline`, `hygiene`. It emits one machine-parseable line carrying a candidate id, a disposition over the closed set `pick|merge|none`, a per-criterion score, a rationale and — where the merge half survives — the merge set. A deterministic tie-break is stated in the section, because two candidates scoring identically on every criterion is reachable and an undefined tie-break makes the contract non-implementable.

**D4 — what bounds a merge of non-overlapping pieces, stated as the shipped mechanism's own bound and not as a wish.** `bin/land-worktree.sh` is the available mechanism: it composes each worker's claimed paths onto the coordinator branch's current tip and writes a two-parent commit, refusing on a machine-checked path-disjointness violation whose class covers exact equality, directory-prefix containment and ASCII case-fold equality. Its guarantee is **path-level and textual only** — its own header says so — and it does not guarantee semantic or interface independence: one candidate may change a signature another calls from a file it never touched, and both land. That gap is recorded as `interface-independence-gap`, not closed here. The judge's phase position is fixed and never dropped: it runs after the implement fan-out and **before Validate, never after**, since Validate and Review gate on one committed tree per the `both-gates-green` invariant. Where in `skills/run/SKILL.md` a future task inserts that step is explicitly not decided here.

**D5 — what the judge is forbidden to do, as three ids rather than a paragraph.** `writes-code` (it produces no implementation and no fix), `edits-candidates` (it never modifies a candidate's tree or hand-off), and `judges-own-candidate` (it never runs as one of the candidates it scores). The third exists because it is the failure mode a judge role invites: an evaluator that can also produce the artefact it scores has no separation left to lose.

**D6 — the cost model is built from this repository's own recorded corpora, and every input is labelled with how it was obtained.** A new closed label carries each input: `- cost-input: <id> — <recorded|derived> — <value-with-unit> — <source>`, where `recorded` means read verbatim from a committed blob in this repository and `derived` means computed from `recorded` values by an arithmetic the note states. The two words are deliberately **not** the note's saving-confidence words: `measured` there means "observed by this task's own pilot probe", and this task ran no probe, so re-using it would claim an observation that did not happen. Six inputs are closed: `rework-round-cost`, `rounds-to-approve-series`, `engineer-phase-cost`, `judge-pass-proxy`, `heavy-review-task` and `stack-rounds`. Every input's block carries a `- command: ` sub-bullet and a `- measured-at: ` sub-bullet, and every command in the section that names a repository file reads that file's **committed blob** at the named ref — `git show <ref>:<path>`, `git ls-tree <ref>` or `git grep --cached` — never the working tree, whose bytes at read time are not that ref's.

**D7 — the model's shape, and the one parameter that stays honest.** Tier-2's cost is `(N−1)` discarded engineer-phase implementations plus one judge pass over N candidates; its benefit is the expected rework rounds avoided multiplied by the per-round cost. The tier's inequality is therefore stated over new `- break-even: tier2-…` parameters — the one label T-1078 **AC4** permits to grow — and never by adding a second `- break-even-condition:` line, which is frozen at exactly one and belongs to Tier 3. The parameter carrying the tier's entire bet, the expected rework rounds avoided, has **no recorded field behind it** and reads `undetermined`; the honest consequence is that the inequality is evaluated in the only direction the evidence supports — solved for the **threshold** number of rework rounds a task would have to avoid for the tier to break even, and that threshold compared against the recorded `rounds-to-approve` distribution. A threshold above what any recorded task ever spent is a result; inventing a rounds-avoided figure to make the inequality resolve is not.

**D8 — the comparison verdict is one new singleton line, and its asymmetry is stated in it.** A fourth `- recommendation: ` line is illegal (T-1069 **AC7** fixes exactly three), so the comparison lives on a new closed label: `- engineer-phase-verdict: <tier2-parallel-implementations-judge|tier3-work-splitting|neither> — <text>`, exactly one note-wide. Its text carries both confidence words, because the two sides of the comparison are not the same kind of evidence: Tier 3 is `measured` and negative at one small scale, Tier 2 is `modeled` from recorded corpora with its decisive parameter `undetermined`. The verdict is mechanically coupled to the two tiers' own `- recommendation: ` lines so it cannot contradict them, and the coupling is direction-neutral: it forbids a verdict that names a tier whose recommendation still reads `not-yet`, and forbids `neither` unless both do.

**D9 — the note's existing label vocabulary is closed against this task.** Every labelled line already defined in the note's Terms section belongs to T-1069's and T-1078's measurements and to merged criteria that count them. This task therefore adds **no line under any of them** and instead closes seven new ones — `- judge-input:`, `- judge-criterion:`, `- judge-output:`, `- judge-forbidden:`, `- judge-bound:`, `- cost-input:`, `- engineer-phase-verdict:` — while rewriting **in place**, without changing their counts, the two lines whose content this task's model actually moves (`- saving: tier2-parallel-implementations-judge` and `- recommendation: tier2-parallel-implementations-judge`). `- break-even: ` is the single label whose count may grow, and `- follow-up: ` the single one whose count is unconstrained. A half-matching line — one opening with an existing label but not satisfying its frozen grammar — is the specific failure this decision exists to prevent, and **AC12** measures it as a count equality against the branch point rather than as a reading.

**D10 — the `pilot3-` family and every Tier-1 and Tier-3 datum are byte-frozen against this task.** The `pilot3-evidence` region, the four `- break-even:` parameter lines T-1078 wrote, the single `- break-even-condition:` line, the Tier-3 `- saving:` and `- recommendation:` lines, and the Tier-1 pilot's own lines are left exactly as merged. This task re-prices one tier from recorded corpora; it does not restate, re-derive or improve another task's measurements.

Contextual trigger: before a third round of rework on that component starts; where the two disagree, the factual trigger governs. Disposition, decided here rather than at the moment it fires. ## Input space

**Reachable input classes** — what this design, its cost model and its criteria really encounter, and which this task must handle correctly:

1. **N candidate implementations whose diffs touch the same path.** This is the ordinary case, not the exception: two engineers solving one task both edit the file the task is about. The design's answer is that the merge half is unavailable there and the disposition is `pick`, which is why `pick` and not `merge` is the contract's default and why the merge half is the pre-commitment's first drop.
2. **Candidates that tie on every scored criterion.** Reachable whenever two candidates pass the same criteria; the criteria's total rank order plus a stated deterministic tie-break is what makes the contract implementable rather than a coin flip.
3. **A candidate whose `- check:` results cannot be produced at all** — a broken check line, a checker that exits non-zero for an unrelated reason. It scores as failing `ac-pass-completeness` rather than being silently skipped, because a check that could not run is not a check that passed.
4. **Zero candidates worth taking.** The disposition vocabulary carries `none` for exactly this, so the judge is never forced to pick the least bad tree.
5. **A recorded corpus that grows while this task runs.** The reviews corpus gains a record for this very task, and the runs corpus gains spans for it; every cost input is therefore labelled with the ref it was read at and every command reads that ref's committed blob.
6. **A cost input with no recorded field behind it.** The expected rework rounds avoided is exactly this, and its handling — `undetermined`, with the inequality solved for a threshold instead — is the whole reason the model can be honest without an arm.
7. **Board headings that do not follow one shape.** T-1078's own QA rounds appear on the board as `### QA round 1`, `### QA round 3`, `### QA round 4` with round 2 carried in bold prose, so any round count taken from board headings disagrees with one taken from the runs corpus; the `stack-rounds` input is derived from the corpus and says which method produced it.
8. **The stack's refs in either era** — the predecessor branch still resolving, or merged and deleted. The two-arm discriminator handles both, and the abandoned-branch case is disclosed as a route-back rather than papered over.
9. **A note line carrying an em dash, a backticked grammar, or a path with a dot** — the note's own line grammars are the parsed surface here, and this task's criteria plus several merged specs' criteria all read them.
10. **The merged spec corpus growing while this task runs.** Every criterion above is presence-, structure- or delta-shaped for exactly this reason; none compares a declared population figure against a live re-derivation.

**Out-of-scope synthetic extremes** — inputs this spec explicitly declines to protect, named concretely so the boundary is falsifiable:

- **Candidate counts beyond three.** The cost model is stated for `N ≥ 2` and substituted at `N = 2` and `N = 3`. Escalating to four, eight or an arbitrary N is declined, and no claim here is made about degrees the model does not substitute.
- **Empirical judge accuracy.** No judge exists and none runs, so how often a judge would pick the candidate a human would have picked is not measured, not modelled and not estimated. A finding of the form "but the judge might pick wrong" is correct and is precisely why the recommendation is scoped rather than a defect in this task.
- **Statistical power.** Eleven tasks of one repository, of heterogeneous shape, on one machine, support a range and a mean and nothing more. Significance tests, confidence intervals, regressions and repeated-measures designs are declined outright.
- **Adversarial candidates.** A candidate crafted to game a scored criterion — padding tests, gaming a diff-size heuristic — is not modelled; the criteria's ranks are a scoring contract, not a threat model.
- **Semantic or interface independence of merged non-overlapping picks.** The shipped mechanism's guarantee is path-level and textual, its own header says so, and closing that gap is named as a bound rather than attempted here.
- **Token cost and dollar cost.** No vendor price model, no per-instance token accounting, no monetary figure appears anywhere; the recorded telemetry does not isolate a candidate implementation's own token cost from a phase total, and inventing one would be an estimate rather than a reading.
- **Generalisation to other repositories or other task shapes.** Every cost input is this repository's own recorded corpus. What a judge would cost in a repository with different gates, a different review culture or a different rounds distribution is not this model's claim.
- **Re-measuring the harness's concurrency capability.** Whether the harness genuinely runs N concurrent Agent-tool instances is an open item both sibling notes already disclose; this task neither re-tests it nor depends on the answer, since it launches nothing.
- **A general spec-partition grammar.** That is Tier 3's precondition, priced by T-1078's pilot; nothing here designs, requires or implies one.
