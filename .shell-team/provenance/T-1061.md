Two options existed: match the sibling scripts' prefixed convention (readable, but would fail the frozen `-x` checks) or print the bare token (passes the frozen checks, at the cost of a less human-readable stderr line with no free-text explanation). The frozen criteria's own literal grammar decided it — this is not a stylistic preference against the sibling convention, it is what AC4/5/6/7 as written require.
