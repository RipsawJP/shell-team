- decision: chose `judge-pass-proxy` as the eleven-task `qa-verifier` `duration_ms` sum divided by the eleven-task total qa-round count (34142822 ÷ 43 ≈ 794019 ms, one round's average) rather than the per-task total (34142822 ÷ 11 ≈ 3103893 ms, all of a task's own QA rounds combined).
  reason: the judge reads N candidate diffs once and emits one verdict — the closest analogue is a *single* reading-and-scoring pass, not a whole task's cumulative QA activity across however many rounds that task happened to need (which conflates the judge's own one-shot cost with a task's own rework history, a different question this note's `rework-round-cost` input already prices separately). Dividing by the round count (43) rather than the task count (11) keeps the proxy scoped to "one pass," consistent with the Notes for engineer's own framing ("the recorded `qa-verifier` per-task wall clock is the closest git-tracked analogue of a reading-and-scoring pass") read as "per round" rather than "per task," and is disclosed explicitly as a proxy with two named limits (not scaled by N; a QA round reads one candidate's diff against ACs, while a judge pass reads N) rather than presented as an exact judge cost.
  grounding: `docs/loop-engineering/agent-concurrency.md` line 45 (`sum_qa=34142822`, n=11) and line 51 (`43 total qa-verifier rounds`); `.shell-team/specs/T-1079-tier2-judge.md` Notes for engineer ("The `judge-pass-proxy` input is a proxy and must be labelled one... its limits belong beside it").
- decision: engineer-phase-verdict reads `neither` (not `tier2-parallel-implementations-judge`), with the tier2 recommendation kept at `not-yet` (reworded, carrying `cost model`), even though the worked substitution's own break-even threshold (≈0.739 rounds at N=2, ≈0.928 rounds at N=3) sits below every value the recorded `rounds-to-approve-series` has ever taken (range 1–9) — a numerically favorable result for tier2.
  reason: a favorable threshold is an arithmetic property of the chosen recorded inputs, not evidence that a real judge would ever clear it — whether a judge's own pick decisions avoid rework at any positive rate is `tier2-rework-rounds-avoided`, which has no recorded field behind it and stays `undetermined`, and the spec's own Non-goals declines empirical judge accuracy outright ("A finding of the form 'but the judge might pick wrong' is correct and is precisely why the recommendation is scoped rather than a defect in this task"). Naming `tier2-parallel-implementations-judge` as the verdict, or moving its own recommendation off `not-yet`, would assert exactly the kind of unearned transfer (a modeled number standing in for a measured one) this note's own saving-confidence vocabulary exists to prevent. `neither` is also the only verdict directly consistent with both tiers' own recommendation lines without contradiction — T-1069 **AC7** (unchanged) fixes `tier3-work-splitting` at `not-yet` (frozen, D10), and **AC11**'s own coupling forbids naming `tier3-work-splitting` as the verdict while its recommendation reads `not-yet`; naming `tier2-parallel-implementations-judge` would have required this task to also move that tier's recommendation off `not-yet`, which the undetermined decisive parameter does not license honestly.
  grounding: `docs/loop-engineering/phase-multiplexing.md`'s own `- engineer-phase-verdict: neither — ` line and its immediately preceding `## Tier-2 judge design and cost comparison` Worked substitution block (this task's own edit); `.shell-team/specs/T-1079-tier2-judge.md` **AC11** (the direction-neutral coupling) and Non-goals ("Empirical judge accuracy... not measured, not modeled, not estimated").
- decision: labelled `engineer-phase-cost` as `recorded` (271772 ms, mean of T-1078's own serial-arm per-instance runtimes 303524 and 240020), rather than `derived`, even though the value itself is an arithmetic mean of two numbers.
  reason: this note's own `cost-input` confidence vocabulary defines `recorded` as "read verbatim from a committed blob" and `derived` as "computed from `recorded` values by an arithmetic this note states" — the two source figures (303524, 240020) are each read verbatim from the frozen `pilot3-evidence` region, and a mean of two directly-observed numbers from the same committed source is the ordinary way this note's own sibling sections already report such figures (e.g. the note's own pre-existing `serial-n1` arm mean is reported without a separate `derived` label). Reserving `derived` for a genuinely cross-source computation (as `judge-pass-proxy` above, which divides one input by a different input) keeps the two words distinguishing "one committed number, possibly averaged over its own reps" from "an arithmetic combination of otherwise-unrelated recorded quantities," rather than making every mean anywhere in the model `derived`.
  grounding: `docs/loop-engineering/phase-multiplexing.md`'s own `- cost-input: engineer-phase-cost — recorded — ` line (this task's own edit) and its cited source, the `pilot3-evidence` region's `per-instance agent runtimes` line (byte-frozen, unedited); the Terms section's own new "Cost-input confidence words" definitions (this task's own edit).
