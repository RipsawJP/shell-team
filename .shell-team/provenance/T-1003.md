- decision: The new scrum-master Loop step that reads the interventions files is inserted as step 5 — after "read the lessons log" and before "compose Keep/Problem/Try" — rather than appended near the end of the Loop (e.g. as a final housekeeping step after Lesson 候補 composition), and the two `steps 2–4` cross-references (the 罠の点検 grounding-material citations) are updated to `steps 2–5` at both sites rather than left naming only the pre-existing four.
  reason: The interventions material is grounding for the Keep/Problem/Try and 罠の点検 sections the agent composes afterward, exactly like the cycle window, review artifacts and lessons log already are — placing the read step before composition (not after) keeps the Loop's own step order consistent with what "grounded in what you already read in steps N–M" actually claims. Appending it at the end instead would have left the interventions material read too late to ground the very sections the 罠の点検 cross-reference names, or required the cross-reference to enumerate a non-contiguous step range.
  grounding: docs/specs/T-1003-retro-reads-interventions.md (AC16: "the Loop's two cross-references to the grounding steps are updated so they stay true after the insertion"); agents/scrum-master.md Loop steps 2–5 and the two "steps 2–5" citations.

- decision: `tests/check-retro/run.sh`'s new `assert_violations` helper counts a fixture's declared violations by taking `grep -c '.'` over the checker's captured stderr (guarded with an explicit `[ -z "$err" ] && n=0` branch), rather than piping stderr through `wc -l` directly or having `bin/check-retro.sh` itself emit a machine-readable violation-count line.
  reason: `wc -l` counts newlines, so a captured stderr string with no trailing newline (the shape `err="$(cmd)"` command substitution always produces, since it strips the trailing newline) undercounts by one on every non-empty case and returns 1 (not 0) on a genuinely empty string — both wrong for this helper's purpose. `grep -c '.'` counts non-blank lines directly and reports 0 on empty input with no special-casing beyond guarding the truly-empty-string case where `printf | grep` itself would emit a spurious blank line. Adding a count line to `check-retro.sh`'s own output was rejected as out of scope: the spec's Non-goals forbid parsing or tallying inside a *different* script (`bin/retro-inputs.sh`'s relationship to interventions files), and by the same reasoning this task does not widen `check-retro.sh`'s output contract just to make its own test suite's counting easier — the test counts what the checker already emits to stderr.
  grounding: docs/specs/T-1003-retro-reads-interventions.md AC14 ("the exact number of violation lines the checker emits"); tests/check-retro/run.sh (`assert_violations` implementation).
