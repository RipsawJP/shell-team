- decision: Tier B (single-entry ablation) is scoped to the engineer role, reusing the committed tier-2 subject as the only fixed task set, and the pilot and full-sweep costs are labelled estimates built from sampled telemetry means.
  reason: The other three roles have no fixed subject, so covering them would need new infrastructure; building the figures from the last two hundred span rows, of which only part carry numeric tokens, makes any product an estimate.
  grounding: `.shell-team/specs/T-1199-playbook-measurement-design.md` D2 and `## Notes for engineer` (Reuse candidates, Cost lines).

- decision: A `not-elicited` verdict (control arm shows no failure) is distinct from `no-longer-needed candidate` and stays `unknown`.
  reason: Without a block-free control arm that shows the failure shape, a zero in the other arms cannot tell a redundant lesson from an untested one.
  grounding: `.shell-team/specs/T-1199-playbook-measurement-design.md` D4 (the retirement-schema gap) and `## Goal` (what counts as still-prevents versus no-longer-needed).

- decision: Rework round 1 sets nine runs per arm, derived from a 0.95 detection bound at a stated minimum effect of 0.3, replacing three repetitions, and prices runs primarily from the committed subject baseline with the telemetry mean as a secondary range.
