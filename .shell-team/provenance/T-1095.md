# T-1095 decision provenance

<!-- BEGIN provenance: T-1095 -->
- decision: the canonical block's four core sentences are phrased as one continuous, unhedged assertion in the loop's own voice (no "the rule is that…", no attributed or conditional framing) for each of the three firing points, the disposition rule, the four-case exception set, and the audit-not-gating clause — rather than, for example, phrasing them as a numbered rule list or splitting the disposition-executes clause across two sentences.
  reason: AC2/AC4/AC5/AC6 fix the exact substrings that must appear and the three negation shapes that must not, but the surrounding sentence structure, sentence count and voice were a free choice with more than one workable shape (a numbered-list form, a two-sentence split of the joined disposition clause, a passive-voice rewrite). The spec's own AC2 body and `## Verdict scope` explicitly warn that byte-level fragment matching cannot exclude a framing inversion (a sentence that quotes the norm inside a rejecting clause) — a genuine semantic-assent risk this repository has already measured as reviewer-adversarial in T-1092. Writing the block as a small number of long, declarative, unhedged sentences in the loop's own voice (matching `templates/prompt-blocks/liveness-declaration.md`'s shipped idiom) was chosen specifically to minimize that risk, since a numbered-list or heavily-clause-nested form reads more easily as a framing device.
  grounding: `.shell-team/specs/T-1095-means-ends-reflection.md` **AC2**'s own disclosure paragraph ("What this criterion is, and what it is not") and its cited counterexample from spec-review round 2; `templates/prompt-blocks/liveness-declaration.md` (the one-paragraph-per-line idiom this block follows); `templates/prompt-blocks/means-ends-reflection.md` (the shipped result).
- decision: the optional `reflection: <value>` line is appended as the last content line of the assembled digest, immediately before `=== END DIGEST ===`, rather than immediately after the `trigger:`/`stop-reason:` line near the top.
  reason: two placements were equally legal against AC9's own check (which only asserts the line exists via `grep -qxF`, not its position), but appending at the very end is the only placement that makes "omitting `--reflection` leaves existing stdout byte-identical to the base blob" (AC9's own byte-identity requirement) trivially true by construction — every existing branch of the assembly (`out+=...`) up to that point is completely unchanged, and the new field is a pure suffix. Inserting the line near the top would have required threading a conditional into the middle of the existing `trigger:`/`stop-reason:` branch, raising the chance of an accidental byte change to that pre-existing branch.
  grounding: `bin/rework-digest.sh`'s assembly block (the `out+=$'\n'"=== END DIGEST ==="` line, now preceded by the conditional `reflection:` append); `.shell-team/specs/T-1095-means-ends-reflection.md` **AC9**'s own-invocation byte-identity clause ("With `--reflection` omitted, the script's stdout for a fixed argument set is byte-identical to the branch-point blob's own stdout").
- decision: the new `--reflection` fixture cases in `tests/rework-digest/run.sh` are appended as one contiguous block immediately before the suite's final `printf '\nAll rework-digest assertions passed.\n'` line, rather than interleaved next to the existing `--stop-reason`/`--trigger` fixture blocks they most resemble.
  reason: AC10 requires the golden regeneration to be purely additive (zero removed lines against the branch-point blob, per `git diff --numstat`). Appending strictly after every pre-existing `pass`/`reject` call guarantees every pre-existing golden line stays byte-identical and in the same order, so the diff against the branch-point golden can only ever be a suffix insertion; interleaving the new cases next to their thematically closest existing block would still be logically valid but would (depending on the diff algorithm's alignment) risk being read as a line reordering rather than a pure addition, which is exactly the class AC10 exists to forbid.
  grounding: `.shell-team/specs/T-1095-means-ends-reflection.md` **AC10** ("the golden's diff against its branch-point blob removes zero lines while adding at least one"); `tests/rework-digest/run.sh` (the new block's placement immediately before the final `printf`); `tests/rework-digest/expected-stdout.txt` regenerated and confirmed additive via `git diff --numstat` (7 insertions, 0 deletions, measured before this record was written).
